# Stakeholder Register

| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | Engagement |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Executive sponsor | Outcomes, risk, budget | High | Monthly steering review |
| Product owner | Customer journeys, value | High | Weekly product review |
| Cloud architect | Architecture, standards | High | Design authority |
| Security lead | Threats, controls, compliance | High | Security gates |
| Engineering lead | Delivery quality | High | Daily/weekly delivery cadence |
| Operations/SRE | Availability, support, recovery | Medium | Readiness + incident exercises |
| Finance/procurement | Cost and contracts | Medium | Cost review at gates |
| Customers | Usability, trust, availability | High | Research and acceptance testing |
