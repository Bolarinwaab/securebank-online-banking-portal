# Stakeholder Register

| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | Engagement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Executive sponsor | Outcomes, risk, budget | High | Monthly gate review |
| Product owner | Customer value, scope | High | Weekly backlog review |
| Cloud architect | Architecture and quality | High | Design authority |
| Security lead | Threats, IAM, compliance | High | Security gates |
| Engineering | Delivery | High | Daily delivery coordination |
| SRE/Operations | Reliability and recovery | Medium/High | Readiness reviews |
| Data/Analytics | Reporting and data quality | Medium | Design/release reviews |
| QA | Acceptance and defects | Medium | Test cycles |
| Customers | Usability and trust | Medium | Research/feedback |
