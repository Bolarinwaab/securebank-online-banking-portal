# Incident Response

## Severity

- **SEV-1:** customer transactions or security materially impacted; incident commander engaged immediately.
- **SEV-2:** major degradation with workaround.
- **SEV-3:** limited defect with low customer impact.

## Response

1. Detect and acknowledge.
2. Assign incident commander and technical lead.
3. Stabilize: protect customers and data before restoring availability.
4. Diagnose using logs, metrics and traces.
5. Mitigate or roll back.
6. Communicate status at agreed intervals.
7. Validate recovery.
8. Preserve evidence and complete a blameless post-incident review.

Security incidents additionally follow the organization's approved evidence preservation, notification and regulatory process.
