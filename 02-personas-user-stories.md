# 2. Personas and User Stories

## Persona 1 — Busy professional

A busy customer uses SecureBank primarily from a phone or laptop. They need fast access to balances, recent transactions, bill payments, and transfers without visiting a branch. Their priorities are security, speed, clarity, and availability.

## Persona 2 — Customer-service representative

A customer-service representative supports customers throughout the day. They need controlled access to customer profiles and account information, with strong authorization and auditability. They should not have unrestricted access to transaction execution capabilities.

## User stories

### Balance inquiry

**As a checking-account customer, I want to see my available balance at any time, so that I can make informed spending decisions.**

### Transfer

**As an authenticated customer, I want to transfer funds between eligible accounts, so that I can move money without visiting a branch.**

### Transaction history

**As an account holder, I want to view recent transactions, so that I can monitor activity and identify unexpected transactions quickly.**
