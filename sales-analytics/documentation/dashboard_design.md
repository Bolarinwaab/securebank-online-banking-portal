# Dashboard Design

## Executive layout

**Header:** Enterprise Sales Performance | Period selector | Market | Region | Category | Discount Band

**KPI strip:** Total Revenue | Gross Profit | Profit Margin | Units Sold | Orders | Customers | Revenue YoY | Profit YoY

**Row 2:** Revenue trend (monthly) | Revenue by Discount Band

**Row 3:** Top Countries | Category / Sub-category profitability

**Row 4:** Country x Year management scorecard

## Visual standards
- White cards on a light neutral canvas
- Strong hierarchy: KPI cards first, trends second, diagnostics third
- Blue/green accents for positive performance; red reserved for loss/exception states
- Currency values abbreviated consistently ($K/$M)
- Percentages shown with one decimal place
- Tooltips should expose revenue, profit, margin, units and YoY
- Every dashboard page must display the data provenance/benchmark disclaimer

## Management interaction
Filters should cross-filter every visual. Country drill-down should support Market > Region > Country > State > City. Product drill-down should support Category > Sub-category > Product. Time should support Year > Quarter > Month > Date.
