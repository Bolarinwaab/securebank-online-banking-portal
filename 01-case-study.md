# 1. Case Study — Online Banking Portal

## Brief description

SecureBank is a digital banking portal that allows customers to securely access account information and perform common banking activities from a web or mobile interface. The architecture follows a service-oriented design so that authentication, customer information, accounts, transactions, and analytics can scale independently.

## Main features

- Secure customer authentication
- Account balance and account-detail inquiry
- Transaction history
- Deposit and withdrawal simulation
- Account-to-account transfer simulation
- Beneficiary management
- Customer profile management
- Notifications and audit events
- Operational analytics

## Typical roles

1. **Customer** — views accounts, transactions, profile information, and initiates banking actions.
2. **Customer-service representative** — assists customers and reviews permitted customer/account information.
3. **Bank administrator** — manages service configuration and operational controls.
4. **Platform/SRE engineer** — monitors reliability, security, availability, and disaster recovery.
